## Information

The E-Accom accommodation management system is an initiative by the Intelligent Automation Engineering Center—under the Faculty of Engineering at Prince of Songkla University (PSU)—in collaboration with the university’s Research and Development Office. It consolidates knowledge and experience gained from providing academic and professional services to the tourism sector between 2020 and 2021 into a unified platform. The system encompasses a Property Management System (PMS), a hotel website interface with booking capabilities, a self-check-in system, and a customer relationship management (CRM) module.

The current iteration of E-Accom is its second version, with development beginning in August 2021. As the lead developer, I oversaw the project, which received support from the Department of Industrial Promotion’s Regional Industrial Promotion Centers 10 and 11, as well as Prince of Songkla University. The system was built using Node.js for the backend and Next.js for the frontend. It continues to be developed by the research center and is currently in use at various hotels, particularly in Trang province.

Following the conclusion of my tenure at the Intelligent Automation Systems Research Center, the E-Accom system has continued to be developed by the relevant team. It is now provided as a service by PSU Innovative Engineering Co., Ltd., a company operating under the umbrella of PSU Holding Company.
