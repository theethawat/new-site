## Information

Calving abnormalities in dairy cows are a significant issue that impacts productivity, fetal survival, and the overall health of the animals. Consequently, monitoring and predicting the onset of labor are crucial for effective dairy herd management. In situations where human intervention or specialized equipment is required for delivery, an inability to accurately predict the timing of calving can lead to delays in providing assistance, potentially causing harm to both the cow and the calf. As a result, research into monitoring the behavior of dairy cows during the pre-calving period has gained considerable attention, particularly through the application of computer vision technology. However, given the specific behavioral patterns and management practices found on farms, relying on a single camera may be insufficient to capture complete data; for instance, a cow might move out of the camera's field of view, resulting in incomplete information. Therefore, employing a multi-camera system is a more suitable approach for the continuous and comprehensive monitoring of dairy cow behavior.
